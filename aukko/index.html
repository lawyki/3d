A pavilion for light

Five openings, one long day.

A concrete house on a lake with nothing inside it. Each room is built around a single cut in the wall and what the sun does with it. Scroll to walk in.

01 · Corridor

Morning, cut into slices.

Eight tall slits in the east wall and nothing else. For a few hours after sunrise the hall is ruled with bars of light, and the dust in the air does the rest.

Length21 mSlit600 mmBest seen05:30 to 08:00

02 · Sky room

A square of noon.

The roof is cut open and left that way. The walls are washed with a warm light from the floor, which makes the blue overhead look deeper than it really is.

03 · Colour room

No edges, only colour.

Fog, and one lit opening at the far end. Give it a minute and the corners of the room disappear. Move across the screen to change the colour.

04 · Lake hall

Evening, with nothing in the way.

The last room has no fourth wall. It faces the point on the lake where the midsummer sun goes down, a little before eleven.
