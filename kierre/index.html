About

One hundred and fifty blades, one idea.

The object on this page is the whole identity. It has no fixed shape. It is a set of identical parts and a rule for how they turn, and every part of the conference gets its own arrangement.

Move across it and the blades answer. Press and a wave runs through all of them. Drag to turn it in your hands.

Venue

A turbine hall on the harbour.

Voimala 3 made electricity for the city until 1976. The main room is 80 metres long and 19 high, with the crane still on its rails. Ten minutes by tram from the central station.
