Väre Sea Pool, Hanko

Swim Hours Deep end Visit

Thirty-three metres of filtered Baltic at the end of the east breakwater, kept at 27 degrees and open to the sky from the first of May.

Pick a lane

Three lanes, three speeds. Slow: breaststroke, backstroke, floating. Steady: continuous swimming, keep right. Fast: sets and intervals. Single swim 7 euros, ten swims 56 euros, season 190 euros.

Open six to nine

Every day from 06:00 to 21:00.

The deep end

The floor falls from 1.8 to 3.8 metres under the boards. One-metre springboard, three-metre platform.

Visit

Väre Sea Pool, East Breakwater 4, 10900 Hanko. Season 1 May to 30 September. Book a lane
