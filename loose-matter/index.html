An exhibition about sand, the most used solid on Earth, and everything we make of it.

50

Billion tonnes, every year

That is how much sand and gravel we dig, dredge and pour. Nothing solid is used more.

Piled up, one year of it would make a wall 27 metres high and 27 metres thick, all the way around the equator.

One pile, one billion tonnes

Three rooms.
One material.

Room 1 · Melt

Glass

Seven parts in ten of every window are sand, held near 1,500 °C until it runs clear.

Room 2 · Bind

Concrete

Sand, gravel, cement, water. Nothing people make is made in greater quantity.

Room 3 · Purify

Silicon

The purest quartz is grown into crystal and sliced. Every chip began as a grain.
