01 / 07Lamp

Light has to come from somewhere.

One point throws photons in every direction. The collector catches what it can and lines it up.

02 / 07Condenser

Then everything is aimed at one point.

A single curve bends the whole stream inward. Blue arrives a little before red.

03 / 07Specimen

The specimen sits exactly here.

At the focus the stream is thinner than a hair. Whatever passes through leaves carrying the picture.

04 / 07Objective

The objective catches it before it scatters.

Two elements gather the spreading light and send it on, straight and calm.

05 / 07Prism

A prism turns the corner.

Inside the glass the colours part, meet a face they cannot cross, and leave in lanes. Look in, and the beam seems to run straight on.

06 / 07Tube lens

One last lens sends each photon home.

The stream is drawn to a second point. From there every photon flies to its own place in the picture.

07 / 07Image

And the picture is back.
