Light leaves the lamp as a cone. One curve turns the cone into a beam.

02 / 07Focus

A simple lens cannot agree with itself.

It bends blue harder than red, so each colour crosses at its own point.

03 / 07Correction

Two elements make it white again.

The first steers each colour back onto its ray. The second straightens them out.

04 / 07Prism

A prism takes white apart on purpose.

Every ray opens into the colours it was carrying.

05 / 07Lanes

A second prism holds them in lanes.

Parallel again, side by side, every colour on its own line.

06 / 07White

Two more put them back.

The lanes close, the colours meet, and the beam is white again.

07 / 07Point

Three elements, one point.

One opens the beam, one straightens it, and the last sends every colour to the same place. That is the whole job of a lens. Lyse primes, made in Bergen.
