01 · Surfaces

Every surface has a voice.

Scatter sand on a plate and make it ring. The sand runs away from what moves and settles where the plate is still. Pick a mode and watch it find the lines.

02 · Rooms

Sound is a shape in the air.

Two sources, one room. Where their waves meet in step the room is loud, and a metre to the left it goes quiet. We model that before the concrete is poured.

03 · Listening

Built around the ear.

The cochlea is a spiral that sorts sound by pitch along its length, high notes at the entrance and low notes at the centre. Every room we tune is tuned for it.
