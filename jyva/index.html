Jyvä 01 / 06  Bed
Sand foundry, Helsinki

Objects grown from sand.

Jyvä prints vessels and furniture out of quartz sand, one 0.4 mm layer at a time. This page is a bed of 120,000 grains. Scroll, and it runs.

02  Bind

A layer is spread. A layer is bound.

A blade lays down a sheet of sand. The head crosses it once and wets one outline with binder. The bed drops 0.4 mm and the blade comes back.

0000of 1,500 layers
03  Brush

Then we brush it out.

The piece cures for a day, buried in its own material. Loose sand comes off from the top down, by hand and by air, and falls through the floor to be sieved.

04  Stack

Fifteen hundred drawings, stacked.

Pull the layers apart and every piece is a pile of outlines. There is no mould, so no outline is off limits.

05  Forms

Four forms in the bed this winter.

06  Return

Nothing leaves as waste.

Sand that was never bound goes straight back into the bed. A piece that comes home is crushed, sieved and printed as something else.
