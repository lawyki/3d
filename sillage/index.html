Movement I / Top / 0 to 15 min

A cold, bright opening

Bergamot and pink pepper are the lightest molecules in the formula. They rise first, travel fastest and are gone within a quarter of an hour.

  • BergamotCalabria, cold-pressed peel
  • Pink pepperRéunion, CO₂ extract
  • Aldehyde C-12Trace
Movement II / Heart / 15 min to 4 h

Iris, worn close

Orris butter is pressed from iris root that has dried for three years. On skin it turns soft and powdery and stays near the body, circling it.

  • Orris butterTuscany, root aged 3 years
  • Ambrette seedEcuador
  • Rose absoluteIsparta
Movement III / Base / 4 h onward

What stays low

Benzoin, cedar and a mineral musk are the heaviest notes. They sink, settle into fabric and slip under everything else, and they are still there the next morning.

  • Benzoin resinoidLaos
  • Atlas cedarMorocco
  • Ambroxan4%
