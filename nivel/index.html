J120 rotary joint actuator

Motor, gear stage and bearing in one 87 mm joint.

The J120 holds a 48 V frameless motor, a 5 : 1 planetary stage and a crossed-roller bearing in a single housing, for legged and collaborative robots. Scrolling this page turns its input shaft.

Section A–A

Cut along the axis.

The rotor turns a 24-tooth sun gear. Four 36-tooth planets roll between the sun and a 96-tooth ring fixed in the housing, and carry the output flange round once for every five turns of the rotor.

1 + 96 / 24 = 5900 → 180 rpm
