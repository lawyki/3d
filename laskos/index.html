Paper, pleated for light, sound and walls.

Laskos is a paper mill and pleating workshop in Tervakoski, Finland. We make one thing: sheets that hold a fold. Shades, blinds and acoustic walls are cut from them. Founded 1968. 48 colours. Pitches of 20, 30 and 60 mm.

One sheet, two faces.

Every pleat has a left face and a right face, and we print both. Lean one way and you read the first sheet. Lean the other way and the second is already there.

Shades that glow at the fold.

Cotton fibre lets light through where the sheet turns away from you. A Laskos shade is brightest in its valleys. Shade 30, pendant Ø 300, 140 €. Shade 45, pendant Ø 450, 190 €. Blind, made to width, from 96 € per metre.

Walls that keep a room quiet.

A pleated wall is mostly air. Sound goes into the valleys and little of it finds the way back out. Absorption αw 0.85, class B. Panel 600 × 1200 mm. Depth 60 mm. Fire class B-s1, d0.
