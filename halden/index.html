01Architecture

Five layers on one plate.

The barrel stores the energy, the train carries it, the balance meters it out. Bridges hold each pivot in a ruby from above, the mainplate from below.

02Escapement

Eight ticks a second.

The balance swings, the lever rocks, and the escape wheel is released one tooth at a time. Each release is one tick.

Four wheels from force to time.

Bridges removed. Each wheel drives the pinion of the next, so every step turns faster and pushes more gently.

Finished on every surface.
