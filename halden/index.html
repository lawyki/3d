Halden · Calibre 01

It starts in the dark.

A watch you read by its own light. The hands are showing your time. Scroll to turn it over and open it.

Scroll

Case

Nine millimetres, edge to edge.

Blackened steel that only shows where the light catches it, and a crown you can find without looking.

Back

Turn it over.

The back is held by its own thread. A short turn and it lets go.

Open

Then it opens.

Underneath is everything that makes it run: more than a hundred parts, each one moving or holding something that moves.

Escapement

Eight ticks a second.

The balance swings, the lever rocks, and the escape wheel is released one tooth at a time. Shown at quarter speed.

Going train

Four wheels, force to time.

With the bridges lifted away. Each wheel drives the pinion of the next, turning faster and pushing more gently.
