Calibre 01 · hand-wound

Time, taken apart.

A mechanical movement, 31 millimetres across, that runs on a spring and nothing else. Scroll, and it comes apart in the order a watchmaker would build it.

01 · Layers

Five layers, nothing hidden.

The plate carries everything. The barrel stores the energy, the train passes it on, the balance spends it evenly, and the bridges hold every pivot in a ruby.

02 · Heartbeat

Four swings a second.

The balance swings, the fork rocks, and the escape wheel is let through one tooth at a time. Every tick you hear is a tooth landing on a ruby.
